## Recommended niche

**"True things that feel impossible"**: verified wonders from nature, deep time and space, plus evidence-graded mysteries, under the ODDLIGHT brand.

## Three content directions to test

| Direction | Format | Example hook | Hypothesis |
|---|---|---|---|
| **D1 — Small Impossibles / Still Here** (nature & deep time) | Single astonishing true fact, revealed in layers | "There's a shark alive today that is older than the United States." | Highest shares and saves; most reliable visuals. |
| **D2 — Unsolved** (evidence-graded mysteries) | Case → evidence → best explanation → what's still unknown | "For 72 seconds in 1977, something answered." | Highest completion (open loop); highest comments. |
| **D3 — Thought Experiment / Deep Field** (space & what-if) | Premise → step-by-step consequences → twist | "If the Sun vanished right now, you'd have 8 minutes left to not know." | Highest replays; broadest appeal. |

## Testing strategy (pilot of 10)

- **Split:** D1 × 4, D2 × 3, D3 × 3. Same voice, grade, caption style and length band (30–45 s), so the *direction* is the main variable.
- **Within directions,** rotate hook types: *statement-shock*, *question*, *visual-first (no words for 1.5 s)*, *countdown/number*. Tag each video.
- **Cadence:** 1 video per day per platform, posted at the same local time window (time is recorded but treated as non-causal).
- **Primary metrics (per platform, from real platform data only):**
  1. Early hold (TikTok "viewed vs swiped", YouTube "stayed to watch")
  2. Average % viewed / completion
  3. Shares + saves per 1,000 views
  4. Follows per 1,000 views
- **Decision rule (after 14 days):** a direction is "promising" if it is top on 2 of the 4 metrics on 2 or more platforms. With n ≈ 3–4 per cell this is **directional, not statistically conclusive**. We say so in the report and confirm with the next batch.
- **Re-tests:** re-cut the best video with a different hook (A/B by time-separated repost on YouTube Shorts, which tolerates this better than TikTok).

## First 30 content ideas

Status key: ✅ well-established fact (source still to be cited per script) · ⚠️ needs careful framing or verification before scripting.

### D1 — Nature & deep time
1. ✅ The Greenland shark: centuries-old, dated through its eye lenses (Nielsen et al., *Science*, 2016) — **pilot video**
2. ✅ Tardigrades survived direct exposure to open space (FOTON-M3, 2007)
3. ✅ The "immortal" jellyfish that turns back into a juvenile (*Turritopsis dohrnii*)
4. ✅ Most of an octopus's neurons are in its arms
5. ✅ The mantis shrimp punch that boils water for an instant (cavitation)
6. ⚠️ The "wood wide web": why the tree-communication story is less certain than you heard (Karst et al., 2023)
7. ✅ Pando: the forest that is one single organism
8. ✅ The Arctic tern flies the equivalent of Earth-to-Moon-and-back several times in a lifetime ⚠️ (verify multiplier)
9. ✅ Axolotls regrow limbs and parts of their heart and brain
10. ✅ The bombardier beetle's near-boiling chemical cannon
11. ✅ Hummingbirds nearly "die" every night (torpor)
12. ✅ The lyrebird that imitates camera shutters and chainsaws

### D2 — Unsolved / "Solved"
13. ✅ The Wow! signal (1977): 72 seconds, never repeated
14. ✅ The Voynich manuscript: vellum dated to the early 1400s, text still unread
15. ✅ Tunguska 1908: 2,000 km² of forest flattened, no crater
16. ⚠️ Dyatlov Pass: the 2021 avalanche model (Gaume & Puzrin) and what it doesn't explain
17. ✅ The "Bloop" (1997): the mystery that turned out to be ice
18. ✅ Mary Celeste (1872): a ship found sailing with no one aboard
19. ⚠️ Lake Nyos (1986): the lake that suffocated a valley — handle with respect for the victims
20. ✅ The Antikythera mechanism: a 2,000-year-old computer
21. ⚠️ The Taos Hum: a sound only some people hear
22. ✅ ʻOumuamua: the first interstellar visitor we ever saw

### D3 — Space & thought experiments
23. ✅ If the Sun vanished, you'd have ~8 minutes 20 seconds of not knowing
24. ✅ Walking to the Moon nonstop would take about 9 years
25. ⚠️ A teaspoon of neutron star (verify mass figure and source)
26. ✅ What you'd actually feel falling into Jupiter
27. ⚠️ Voyager 1 is reaching one light-day from Earth — around now (verify the exact date with NASA/JPL; potentially very timely)
28. ✅ What if the Moon disappeared tonight?
29. ✅ The oldest light in the universe is all around you (CMB)
30. ✅ What if Earth stopped spinning for one second?
