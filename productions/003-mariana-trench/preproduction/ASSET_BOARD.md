# Visual reference and asset board: 003-mariana-trench

Status: **PENDING owner review.** The board image (reference next to animatic frame for each beat) is generated locally at `work/preproduction/visual_reference_board.jpg` and sent with the pre-production package. It is not committed, because it contains full reference images.

## Rules applied

- Every frame of the video is generated by code. There is **no stock footage, no AI-generated video and no AI-generated images.**
- Reference images are **guidance only**. They were viewed to inform modelling, colour and lighting, and are never composited, traced, projected or shown in the video.
- Sources are official, scientific or openly licensed. Each image has a metadata file (`references/metadata/ref_0N.json`) recording the source page, file URL, creator, credit line, licence, retrieval date and usage rights. Hashes are pinned in `references/references.json`. Changing an image invalidates exactly the beats that use it.
- Small 320 px previews (`references/previews/`, 120 KB in total) are committed with attribution. Full images live in `references/images/`, which is git-ignored.

## Gaps the references reveal (for the full-render pass)

1. **Realism.** The references are photographs; our frames are stylised procedural 3D. The approved direction says "realistic-looking", and the animatic does not reach that yet. Most visible gaps:
   - the sea surface in b1 is stripy;
   - the lander is a simple tube frame, and the bait bag is a wireframe sphere;
   - the amphipods read as pale grubs (no legs or antennae at this size);
   - the snailfish is a smooth pink cone.
2. **Colour.** ref_03 and ref_06 show cyan-green backscatter and pale greenish sediment; we use neutral grey-blue.
3. **Behaviour.** ref_05 (panel B) shows snailfish gathering at bait. We keep one fish crossing the light, as the beat plan asks.
